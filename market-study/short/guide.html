How to run the five slides

Heaft · market opportunity
~3 min 40 s of speaking
01Problem

“People quit the gym because nobody tells them what to do.”

Picture
One line falling from Day 1 to Month 3: you join → what do I do? → same three machines → gone. Plus 63 of 100. Nothing else on the slide.
The point
They don't quit because it is hard. They quit because they are lost — and being lost is a product problem, so it can be fixed.
If asked
63% — Sperandei 2016, J Sci Med Sport, 5,240 members. If asked whether it is real and not just a statistic: our first three users in Barcelona said exactly this — one could not answer his training goal on day one.
02Who

“Our goal is the beginner — and 3 million of them start every year.”

Picture
A positioning map. Across: just joined → years in the gym. Up: doesn’t know what to do → knows. Red corner = no plan, starts and stops. Bottom-right = years in, same three machines. Top-right = the regulars.
The point
Trace both axes before touching a dot. Then the dots in order: regulars, same three machines, the red box. End on the number, not the box.
If asked
Exact figure is 2.75M — say “nearly three million”. It is 39% of Spain's 7.05M members being new each year (HFA 2025: 33.6% attrition + 5.5% growth).
03How big

“€3B in Europe. €6M is ours.”

Picture
Three circles, outside in. Europe €3B · Spain €300M · ours by year three €6M.
The point
Say the big number, then make it feel easy: €6M is 150,000 subscribers — 5 in every 100 Spanish beginners. And Spain is only 10% of Europe, so the room above us is the story.
If asked
Rounded; exact: Spain 282M, target 5.5M, 137,500 subs. EuropeActive / Deloitte 2026 (75.5M members) · Deloitte / España Activa 2025 (7.05M) · RevenueCat 2026 (39.94 median price). Sources report in USD — the slides show the same figures in euros, not converted.
04Opening

“Running earns €340M a year. The gym is still open.”

Picture
Strava €340M and MyFitnessPal €310M, against three tiny gym bars. Biggest is €40M.
The point
The money in fitness apps is real — it just hasn't been made in the gym yet. Nobody has built the winner.
If asked
Business of Apps (MyFitnessPal) · Sacra (Strava) · BusinessWire (Ladder) · Sensor Tower (Fitbod, Hevy). Rounded; the gym figures are analyst estimates, not company disclosures — say so.
05Advantage

“Three things that matter. One app has all three.”

Picture
A comparison table. Three rows: writes your plan · food follows training · €10 or less. Everyone ticks something; only the Heaft column is green all the way down.
The point
Read across each row out loud. Do not skip the competitors' ticks — a table where only we score looks rigged, and giving them their row is what makes the middle row land. Then stop.
If asked
Prices rounded from the store listings (9.99 / 3.99 / 15.99 / 11.66 / 199), shown in euros. Future's plan is written by a human. Rows from each company's own store listing, Sep 2026.
