The open seat
07 / 14
Why nobody leads the AI-coach category yet

Four reasons the seat is still empty.

1 · "AI" is a label
"AI" is mostly a quiz and a template
Real adaptive engines exist. They are niche and silent: no dialogue, no food.
28% of fitness apps now bid on "AI" keywords, up from 19%. Sensor Tower, Feb 2026.
2 · Split in three
Nobody does all three
$4 apps record. $13–35 apps generate. $199 apps put a person on the phone.
Reviewers call it "algorithmic vs human-first, no dominant leader".
3 · Trust not yet earned
People do not believe an AI coach yet
So it must prove itself in week one, with a real plan, not a chat box.
Only 10% prefer AI guidance to a human coach. Les Mills 2026.
4 · Retention kills everyone
Whoever survives six weeks wins
The category loses almost everyone. The winner keeps beginners.
Day-30 retention 3–4%. First renewal: monthly 57%, annual 25%. RevenueCat H&F, 2026.
R
The proof it can be done: Runna
One behaviour, plans that really re-adjust, $19.99 a month. Strava bought it in April 2025. The gym version does not exist yet.
Sources Sensor Tower, Health & Fitness Apps & AI (Feb 2026) · Les Mills 2026 Global Fitness Report · Athletech News, Future pulls the plug on AI (Jun 2026) · RevenueCat renewal rates by category (Apr 2026) · Business of Apps H&F benchmarks · Strava press release (17 Apr 2025) · SportsPro (Runna price).

The first three users · Barcelona, September 2026

The desk research ends here. We stood next to three people while they used it.

User 1 · day one
He did not know the words
Our intro asks for experience, activity level and goal. His honest answer to all three: "I don't know, I just got here."
We wrote onboarding for people who already train.Change: an intro a beginner can finish.
User 2 · the screen
Our colour reads as an error
He said the screen felt overwhelming, and that the red looked like a warning. It is a collision we built ourselves.
In a gym red is the 25 kg plate. On a screen it means something broke.Change: colour with a job.
User 3 · the cycle tracker
She missed two days
She went to the part built for her, forgot to log for two days, and found the app only accepts today.
A gap that can never be filled makes every prediction after it wrong.Change: log the days you missed.
None of this came from a survey.n = 3· first users, not a sample · every finding is a fault in our own product.

The answer
13 / 14
How we become the first full coach

Nobody owns "the coach" yet. The seat is open, and the maths says we can afford to sit in it.

Bet 1 · Product
Lead with the programme, not the log
A new user sees a generated week before logging anything. Nutrition follows the day. Next week gets harder on its own.
Bet 2 · Price
Coach depth at logger prices
$9.99 a month sits between the loggers ($4) and the AI generators ($13), and 20× under a human coach app ($199). Each Pro still keeps 80%.
Bet 3 · Segment
Win beginners in their first 6 months
The half who quit by month six are the people no logger serves. Barcelona students first, 15 interviews, then the numbers decide.

Built, not finished. The market is real and the margin is real. The first three users showed us exactly where the product still fails a beginner. That is the next three weeks.
